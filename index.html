The idea

TIME IS OUR MOST IMPORTANT INGREDIENT.

We wanted pizza with more character. That meant going back to the dough — real fermentation, careful preparation, hand stretching. We don't treat dough as a shortcut. We give it time.

Flavour starts before the oven. From culture to crust, the long way round to a better slice.
